## Who we are

Inspired Beings EURL is a software engineering company registered in France. This policy covers
this website and the mobile applications we publish, including the Fossling collection.

## This website

This is a static website. It requires no account, uses **no cookies**, and embeds **no
analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind**.

It is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). As with any web host, GitHub may
record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs; see the
[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for details. We have no access to, and collect none of, that data.

## Our mobile applications (Fossling)

Fossling apps are free, open-source, and adless by design. They collect **no personal data**,
contain **no advertising or analytics SDKs**, and **share or sell nothing** — there is nothing
to share.

### Fossling Magnifier

Fossling Magnifier uses your device's camera to display a live magnified image on your screen.

- The **camera permission** is used solely for that purpose. Images are processed on your
  device and never leave it.
- The app requests **no Internet permission**: it is technically unable to transmit any data.
- No account, no cookies, no identifiers, no data collection of any kind.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, and erasure over your personal data.
Since we collect and store no personal data, there is nothing we could look up, hand over, or
delete — but you can always reach us with questions.
