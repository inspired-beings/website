---
title: "FAQ"
lead: "Answers to what prospective clients ask us most — if yours isn't here, just ask."
groups:
  - title: "Getting started"
    items:
      - q: "How do we start working together?"
        a: "Book a free 30-minute discovery call. We'll ask about your product, constraints and timeline, then follow up with a recommended plan and a fixed-scope proposal — no obligation to proceed."
      - q: "Do you work with early-stage startups, or only established companies?"
        a: "Both. Our Starter plan is built for lean, early-stage budgets; Standard and Enterprise suit teams that need a dedicated, ongoing partner."
      - q: "Can you work alongside our existing in-house team?"
        a: "Yes — most engagements plug into an existing codebase and team. We're often brought in for a specific gap: extra senior capacity, a security audit, or a migration nobody wants to own alone."
  - title: "Pricing & engagements"
    items:
      - q: "What's included in the monthly price?"
        a: "Engineering time on your project, our standard security and accessibility review pass, and async support through your preferred channel. Anything outside scope is quoted separately before we start it."
      - q: "Is there a minimum contract length?"
        a: "Our published plans assume a three-month minimum, which is enough time to properly ramp up and deliver something real. Shorter, fixed-scope audits (security, accessibility) don't carry that minimum."
      - q: "Can we change plans or cancel later?"
        a: "Yes. You can move between plans, add or remove capacity, or convert to a lighter maintenance retainer once you're live — with 30 days' notice, no penalty."
  - title: "Process & delivery"
    items:
      - q: "How often do we hear from you during a project?"
        a: "A written update every week at minimum, plus a working demo at every milestone. We'd rather over-communicate early than surprise you at the end."
      - q: "What happens if our priorities change mid-project?"
        a: "We replan. Fixed-scope doesn't mean frozen-scope — we'll show you the trade-off in time or budget before anything changes, and you decide."
      - q: "Who owns the code and infrastructure once we're done?"
        a: "You do, entirely. Source, documentation and infrastructure access transfer to your team at handover — there's no vendor lock-in built into what we ship."
  - title: "Security, accessibility & compliance"
    items:
      - q: "Is accessibility really included by default, or is it an add-on?"
        a: "Included by default. Every interface we ship targets WCAG 2.2 AA as a baseline, not a retrofit ordered after a complaint."
      - q: "Do you sign NDAs and data processing agreements?"
        a: "Yes, as standard practice before any code or credentials change hands — ask your point of contact and we'll send ours or review yours."
      - q: "How do you handle security vulnerabilities you find in our systems?"
        a: "We disclose them to you privately and immediately, with a suggested fix and severity rating — never publicly, and never held back for a later report."
