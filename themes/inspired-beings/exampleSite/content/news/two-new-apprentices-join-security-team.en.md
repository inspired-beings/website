We're welcoming two apprentices into the security and accessibility audit team this term, both finishing degrees in applied cryptography and human-computer interaction respectively.

<!--more-->

Their first project: shadowing a full accessibility audit end to end, then running the next one themselves under review — the same "watch one, do one, teach one" progression the rest of the team came up through.

Apprenticeships here run a full year, paid, with a mentor assigned from day one. We keep hiring this way because most of our current senior staff joined the same route.
