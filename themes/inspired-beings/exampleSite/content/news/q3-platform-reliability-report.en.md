Every quarter we publish the same three numbers, whether they flatter us or not: uptime, median incident resolution time, and the count of incidents that reached a customer before we noticed them ourselves.

<!--more-->

This quarter: 99.97% uptime across managed services, a median resolution time of 11 minutes (down from 19), and zero customer-first detections — every incident was caught by our own monitoring before a client had to tell us.

The improvement mostly comes down to boring work: better alert thresholds, fewer false positives, and runbooks that people actually trust enough to follow without waiting for a human to confirm. None of it is glamorous, and that's rather the point.

We'll keep publishing this even in a quarter where the numbers are worse. That's the deal.
